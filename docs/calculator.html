How towns pay: at $1,000/month the service costs $12,000/year — below most municipal RFP thresholds. Funding channels include NHTSA highway-safety grants through state safety offices ($800M+ approved for FY2026, impaired-driving countermeasures included) and offender cost recovery: Grass Valley, CA already charges DUI offenders $2.00 per minute of police response, which at ~37 DUIs/year recovers roughly $13,300/year — enough to cover this fee on its own.

Proof it works: Wisconsin's Road Crew ride-home program cut alcohol-related crashes 17% in three rural communities at $15,300 per avoided crash, vs. an average alcohol-crash cost of ~$56,000. The Tavern League of Wisconsin SafeRide program has given 1M+ rides since 2004.

Sources: FBI Uniform Crime Reporting (2021); US Office of Justice Programs / NIJ, "Public Costs of DUI Processing: A Study in the California Municipal Courts"; Washington State Caseload Forecast Council Criminal Justice Cost Model (2023); University of Wisconsin / WisDOT Road Crew evaluation; City of Grass Valley fee schedule FY2024-25; NHTSA.
