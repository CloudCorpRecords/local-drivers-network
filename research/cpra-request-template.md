# Public Records Request Template (California CPRA)

*Working template for the V2.0 records step. General information, not legal advice. Adapt per state — fee and response rules differ outside California (see Brief Appendix A.3).*

## Rules baked into this template
1. **Existing records only.** The CPRA requires agencies to produce records that exist; it does not require them to answer questions, run studies, or create new statistics. Ask for source records; compute the answers ourselves.
2. **Electronic format, email delivery.** Existing electronic records are commonly produced at $0; paper triggers per-page duplication fees ($0.10–$0.25/page typical).
3. **Fee cap in every request.** No surprise bills, ever: the template instructs the agency to stop and quote before exceeding $25.
4. **File in the project's own name.** California has no special fee category for local records and no purpose test — anyone may request on the same terms, so no special status is needed or claimed.
5. **Log everything** in the `data/screening-template.csv` format: agency, date, fee quoted, fee paid, what was received.

## Template

> Subject: California Public Records Act Request — DUI Arrest and Response Records
>
> Dear Records Custodian,
>
> Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request copies of the following existing records for the most recent three full calendar years available:
>
> 1. The total number of driving-under-the-influence (DUI) arrests made by your agency per year, in any existing report, log, or summary form.
> 2. Any existing records showing the average or typical officer time spent processing a DUI arrest (for example, existing time studies, cost-recovery schedules, or fee schedules for DUI response).
> 3. Your agency's current fee schedule for DUI investigation/response cost recovery, if one exists.
> 4. Any existing summary of alcohol-involved traffic collisions within your jurisdiction per year.
>
> I request that records be provided in electronic format (PDF or spreadsheet) by email to [EMAIL]. If any record exists only on paper, please provide an estimate of duplication costs before copying.
>
> **Fee instruction:** If the total fees for this request will exceed **$25**, please do not proceed; instead, notify me with an itemized estimate so I may narrow the request. I understand fees are limited to the direct cost of duplication under the Act.
>
> If any portion of this request is unclear, please contact me so I can clarify rather than delay processing. Thank you for your assistance.
>
> [NAME] · [ORGANIZATION] · [EMAIL] · [PHONE] · [DATE]
